Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Chatify AI

Is Chatify AI free? +
Yes! Chatify AI has a free tier that gives you access to 2 AI models and 10 daily searches. Our Pro plan ($15/month) unlocks all models, unlimited searches, file uploads, and priority support.
Which AI models are supported? +
We support Mythical (advanced reasoning), Sonet (balanced), and Chatify (fast). On the free plan, you get access to Chatify and Sonet. Pro plan includes Mythical for deep research. We're continuously optimizing model performance.
Is my research private? +
Absolutely. Your research is end-to-end encrypted. We never sell your data or use it for training. You can delete your research anytime. Privacy is our top priority.
Do I need technical knowledge to use it? +
No. Chatify AI is designed for everyone. You type a question or upload a document, and the AI handles the rest. No coding or technical skills required.
Can I upload files? +
Yes! Pro plan members can upload PDFs, images, and documents. The AI will analyze them and answer your questions about the content.
How can I contact support? +
Email us at support@chatify.ai or use the chat widget in the app. Pro plan members get priority support with responses in under 2 hours.
