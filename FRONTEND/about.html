About Chatify AI

Built by Legend Inc. to make AI research accessible to everyone.

Our mission

We believe that access to advanced AI tools should not be a privilege. Chatify AI was created to democratize AI research, enabling students, professionals, and teams to ask better questions, find better answers, and make better decisions.

Our story

Legend Inc. was founded in 202x6 by a team of AI researchers and product builders who saw the gap between cutting-edge AI capabilities and practical accessibility. We started Chatify AI to bridge that gap—creating a platform that combines the power of multiple AI models with the simplicity of a single, intuitive interface.

Today, Chatify AI helps thousands of researchers, students, and professionals accelerate their work and unlock new insights.
